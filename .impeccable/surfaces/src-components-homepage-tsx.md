## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet bench where the work is laid out as specimens. One big figure at a time, lots of air, no frames, no night. Refuses: dark room, viewfinder brackets, orange signal, bordered header, a grid of six small plates.

OWN-WORLD: Cool daylight laboratory. Ground #eef1f2 (blue-grey white, not cream), ink near-black blue #10171c, one cold accent ultramarine #2b45e0 used for the lead dots and the current link only. Geologica light for display, Onest for text, JetBrains Mono for readouts only. Hairlines, 0 radius controls, no shadows. Dark is an opt-in choice (slate #0e1317, accent #93a6ff).

STORY: A visitor sees a huge light-weight ZorahM next to a calm Saturn, understands it is a personal bench of experiments, and drifts down a specimen viewer to whichever scene draws them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: tiny mark and nav in one unbordered row. Title ZorahM at ~18vw, weight 300, bottom-left; lead text and the single action under it. Right: Saturn plate at ~58vw, ink dots, ultramarine accent share, no frame, bleeding off the right edge.

FORM: Pinned by the user (calm laboratory, light). No concept-seed roll. Index is a specimen viewer: names listed large on the left, one big plate on the right that morphs to the hovered or focused section. Seed key: pinned-by-user.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
